Professionalism First

Elevating the "Kost-Kostan" Experience

Founded on the principle of corporate modernism, Teridox Property specializes in the management of high-utility residential spaces. We recognized a gap in the market where traditional "kost-kostan" often lacked the transparency and structural reliability of modern enterprises.

Our journey began with a single property and a commitment to architectural integrity. Today, we manage a diverse portfolio of units, ensuring that every tenant experiences the warmth of residential living balanced with the efficiency of a high-end SaaS platform.

We leverage a strict 8px spacing system in our operational layouts—both physical and digital—to manage cognitive load and ensure a seamless experience for both owners and residents.
