Booking & Leasing

How do I schedule a property viewing?

expand_more

Scheduling a viewing is simple. Navigate to the 'Listing Unit' page, select the property you're interested in, and click the 'Book a Tour' button. You'll be able to choose a date and time that fits your schedule, and one of our property agents will confirm the appointment within 24 hours.

What documents are required for the application?

expand_more

Standard applications require a government-issued ID, proof of income (last 3 months' payslips or tax returns), and references from previous landlords. All documents can be uploaded directly through our secure Tenant Portal once you start the application process.

Payments & Billing

When is monthly rent due?

expand_more

Rent is typically due on the 1st of each month. We offer a 3-day grace period, after which a late fee may be applied as per your lease agreement. You can set up automated recurring payments through the Owner or Tenant Portal to avoid late fees.

Can I pay rent with a credit card?

expand_more

Yes, we accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through our portal. Please note that credit card transactions may incur a small processing fee. Direct bank transfers (ACH) are free of charge.

Facilities & Maintenance

How do I submit a maintenance request?

expand_more

All non-emergency maintenance requests should be submitted via the Tenant Portal. Include photos and a detailed description to help our team address the issue faster. For emergencies (water leaks, gas smell, lockouts), please call our 24/7 hotline immediately.

House Rules

What is the policy on pets?

expand_more

Most of our properties are pet-friendly, though weight and breed restrictions may apply. A non-refundable pet deposit and a small monthly pet rent are standard. Please refer to your specific lease agreement or contact your property manager for site-specific rules.
